# Demo maps playtest — findings (2026-11-08)

Automated playtest with `tools/playtest_demo_maps.tscn` (25 seeds × 6 maps × Normal/Hard × two player
styles; numbers in `demo_maps_report.md`, regenerated by the tool). Both sides are bots, so this
measures the maps and the rules, not human play. Stats: F-ELV (enemy levels), hit_base 70.

## Bugs found and fixed in this pass

1. **Enemy AI never moved and attacked in one turn** (since milestone 1). `EnemyAI.plan_unit` took
   its standing cells from `RangeHandler.compute_attack_cells()`, which only returns the threat ring
   *outside* the move area, so every "move here and attack" plan was illegal; the director refused
   the move and the unit waited where it stood. On demo_01 nobody fought for 30 turns. Fixed: standing
   cells are the cells the unit can reach.
2. **Escort NPC froze at the water** (demo_02 was unwinnable). `NpcAI.plan_walk` (and the enemy
   approach) chose the reachable cell with the smallest straight-line distance, which stops in front
   of the 2-tile water band. Fixed: new `MovementHandler.distance_field()` / `closest_by_path()` —
   the walk follows the real terrain path.

Regression test: `test_ai_pathing_regressions`. The ch01 full-battle smoke now ends when the three
target soldiers fall (the commander may survive), so its kill check became "at least 3".

## Observations for the designer (not changed — design decisions)

1. **Escort NPC vs. our units.** NPCs cannot walk through player units (they are a different side),
   so a player standing in the corridor blocks the escort. Should escort NPCs pass through the
   player's units like allies do?
2. **Hard plays exactly like Normal on these maps.** F-DIFF multiplies enemy stats by 1.08, which
   floors back to the same value on roster-scale stats (Str 8 × 1.08 = 8.64 → 8, Def 5 → 5).
   Nightmare (× 1.15) adds +1 to a few stats. Options: round instead of floor, bigger multipliers,
   or the Fire Emblem way — enemies +N levels on Hard (fits F-ELV).
3. **demo_03 (persuade) is very hard to finish cleanly.** Threshold 2 with three guards that attack
   the squad: counters alone often kill a third guard, and a bot that never attacks the faction
   still triggers the bad ending in 9 of 13 wins. Options: raise the threshold, make the river guards
   hold back (Protect/Persuade AI), or fewer guards.
4. **The other five maps are short and safe** (2 enemies each, won in 2–4 turns, no unit lost) —
   fine for a tutorial slice, too easy for anything else.
